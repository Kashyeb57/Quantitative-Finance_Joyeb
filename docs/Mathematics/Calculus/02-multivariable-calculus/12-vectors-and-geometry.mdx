### 12.1 Three-Dimensional Coordinate System

**3D Coordinate System:** A point $P$ is represented by an ordered triple $(x, y, z)$.

**Distance between two points $P_1(x_1, y_1, z_1)$ and $P_2(x_2, y_2, z_2)$:**
$$
d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2 + (z_2 - z_1)^2}
$$

**Equation of a Sphere** (center $(a,b,c)$, radius $r$):
$$
(x - a)^2 + (y - b)^2 + (z - c)^2 = r^2
$$

### 12.2 Vectors

A vector has **MAGNITUDE** and **DIRECTION**.

**Vector representation:**
$$
\mathbf{a} = \langle a_1, a_2, a_3 \rangle
$$

**Magnitude (length):**
$$
|\mathbf{a}| = \sqrt{a_1^2 + a_2^2 + a_3^2}
$$

**Unit vector** (magnitude $= 1$):
$$
\hat{\mathbf{u}} = \frac{\mathbf{a}}{|\mathbf{a}|}
$$

### 12.3 The Dot Product

$$
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = a_1 b_1 + a_2 b_2 + a_3 b_3 = |\mathbf{a}| |\mathbf{b}| \cos(\theta)
$$
*(where $\theta$ is the angle between the vectors)*

**GEOMETRIC MEANING:**
- $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} > 0 \implies \theta < 90^\circ$ (acute — same general direction)
- $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 0 \implies \theta = 90^\circ$ (perpendicular/orthogonal)
- $\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} < 0 \implies \theta > 90^\circ$ (obtuse — opposite general directions)

**Projection of $\mathbf{b}$ onto $\mathbf{a}$:**
$$
\text{proj}_{\mathbf{a}}(\mathbf{b}) = \left( \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{|\mathbf{a}|^2} \right) \mathbf{a}
$$

### 12.4 The Cross Product

$$
\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = \langle a_2 b_3 - a_3 b_2, \; a_3 b_1 - a_1 b_3, \; a_1 b_2 - a_2 b_1 \rangle
$$

> **KEY PROPERTY:** $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}$ is **PERPENDICULAR** to both $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$.

```text
       z  a × b
       |↑
       |/
  ─────+──────> a
      /|
     / |
    b  |y
```

**Magnitude:**
$$
|\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}| = |\mathbf{a}| |\mathbf{b}| \sin(\theta) = \text{area of parallelogram}
$$

**Properties:**
- **Right-hand rule:** curl fingers from $\mathbf{a}$ to $\mathbf{b}$, thumb points to $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}$
- $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b} = -(\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{a})$ (anti-commutative)

### 12.5 Lines and Planes

**LINE through $P_0(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ with direction $\mathbf{v} = \langle a, b, c \rangle$:**
- **Vector form:** $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{r}_0 + t\mathbf{v}$
- **Parametric:** $x = x_0 + at, \quad y = y_0 + bt, \quad z = z_0 + ct$

**PLANE through $P_0$ with normal $\mathbf{n} = \langle a, b, c \rangle$:**
- **Scalar equation:** $a(x - x_0) + b(y - y_0) + c(z - z_0) = 0$
- **General form:** $ax + by + cz = d$

```text
         z
         |   n ↑ (normal vector)
         |   |
         |   |
         +───────────────> y    ← plane (flat surface)
        /
       x
```
