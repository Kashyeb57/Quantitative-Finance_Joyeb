### 9.1 What is a Differential Equation?

An equation involving a function AND its derivatives.

**ORDER** = highest derivative present.

**Examples:**
- $y' = ky$ (1st order — population growth)
- $y'' + \omega^2y = 0$ (2nd order — oscillation)
- $\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = k \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}$ (PDE — heat equation)

**Euler's Method** — numerical approximation:

$$
y_{n+1} = y_n + h \cdot F(x_n, y_n)
$$

Start at $(x_0, y_0)$ and take steps of size $h$. Each step follows the local tangent slope, so the straight-line Euler path drifts below the true (curving) solution — error grows with each step.

### 9.3 Separable Equations

**Form:** $\frac{dy}{dx} = g(x) \cdot h(y)$

**Separate variables:**
$$
\frac{1}{h(y)} \, dy = g(x) \, dx
$$

**Integrate both sides:**
$$
\int \frac{1}{h(y)} \, dy = \int g(x) \, dx
$$

**Example:** $\frac{dy}{dx} = xy$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{dy}{y} &= x \, dx \\
\int \frac{dy}{y} &= \int x \, dx \\
\ln|y| &= \frac{x^2}{2} + C \\
y &= A e^{x^2/2}
\end{aligned}
$$

### 9.4 Logistic Growth

$$
\frac{dP}{dt} = kP\left(1 - \frac{P}{M}\right), \qquad P(t) = \frac{M}{1 + A\,e^{-kt}}
$$

where $M$ is the **carrying capacity** (maximum sustainable population). Growth is fastest at the inflection point $P = M/2$ and slows to zero as $P$ approaches the asymptote $M$.

### 9.5 First-Order Linear ODEs

**Form:** $y' + P(x)y = Q(x)$

**SOLUTION METHOD — Integrating Factor:**
$$
I(x) = e^{\int P(x) dx}
$$

Multiply both sides by $I(x)$:
$$
[I(x) \cdot y]' = I(x) \cdot Q(x)
$$

Integrate both sides:
$$
I(x) \cdot y = \int I(x) \cdot Q(x) \, dx
$$

Solve for $y$.

### 9.6 Predator-Prey Systems (Lotka-Volterra)

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{dx}{dt} &= ax - bxy && \text{(prey — grows alone, dies with predator)} \\
\frac{dy}{dt} &= -cy + dxy && \text{(predator — dies alone, grows with prey)}
\end{aligned}
$$
