### 3.1 Basic Differentiation Rules

| Rule | Formula |
|---|---|
| **Constant** | $\frac{d}{dx}[c] = 0$ |
| **Power** | $\frac{d}{dx}[x^n] = nx^{n-1}$ |
| **Constant Multiple** | $\frac{d}{dx}[c \cdot f(x)] = c \cdot f'(x)$ |
| **Sum** | $\frac{d}{dx}[f(x) + g(x)] = f'(x) + g'(x)$ |
| **Difference** | $\frac{d}{dx}[f(x) - g(x)] = f'(x) - g'(x)$ |
| **Exponential** | $\frac{d}{dx}[e^x] = e^x$ |
| **General Exp** | $\frac{d}{dx}[b^x] = b^x \cdot \ln(b)$ |

### 3.2 Product and Quotient Rules

**Product Rule:**
$$
\frac{d}{dx}[f(x) \cdot g(x)] = f'(x) \cdot g(x) + f(x) \cdot g'(x)
$$

> **Memory Trick**: "first times derivative of second plus second times derivative of first"

**Example:**
$$
\frac{d}{dx}[x^2 \cdot \sin(x)] = 2x \cdot \sin(x) + x^2 \cdot \cos(x)
$$

**Quotient Rule:**
$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\right] = \frac{f'(x) \cdot g(x) - f(x) \cdot g'(x)}{[g(x)]^2}
$$

> **Memory Trick**: "low d-high minus high d-low, over low squared"

**Example:**
$$
\frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{\sin(x)}{x}\right] = \frac{\cos(x) \cdot x - \sin(x) \cdot 1}{x^2} = \frac{x \cos(x) - \sin(x)}{x^2}
$$

### 3.4 The Chain Rule

**The most important rule in calculus.**

If $y = f(u)$ and $u = g(x)$, then:
$$
\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy}{du} \cdot \frac{du}{dx} = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)
$$

**VISUAL — think of it as gears:**
$$ x \xrightarrow{g} u \xrightarrow{f} y $$
> Rate of change of $y$ w.r.t $x$ = (rate $y$ changes per $u$) $\times$ (rate $u$ changes per $x$)

**Examples:**
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{d}{dx}[\sin(x^2)] &= \cos(x^2) \cdot 2x \\
\frac{d}{dx}[e^{3x}] &= e^{3x} \cdot 3 \\
\frac{d}{dx}[(x^2+1)^5] &= 5(x^2+1)^4 \cdot 2x
\end{aligned}
$$

**Nested chain rule:**
$$
\frac{d}{dx}[\sin(e^{x^2})] = \cos(e^{x^2}) \cdot e^{x^2} \cdot 2x
$$

> **Quant Finance:** Chain rule is THE fundamental tool in derivatives pricing. Itô's lemma (stochastic chain rule) derives Black-Scholes PDE.

### 3.5 Implicit Differentiation

Sometimes y is not solved explicitly for x. Differentiate both sides and treat y as y(x):

**Example:** $x^2 + y^2 = 25$ (circle of radius 5)

<HandPlot
  title="Implicit curve: x² + y² = 25"
  xLabel="x" yLabel="y"
  domain={[-5, 5]}
  range={[-5.5, 5.5]}
  curves={[
    { f: (x) => Math.sqrt(25 - x*x), color: 'ink', label: 'upper: y = +√(25−x²)' },
    { f: (x) => -Math.sqrt(25 - x*x), color: 'ink', label: 'lower: y = −√(25−x²)' },
  ]}
  points={[{ x: 3, y: 4, label: 'P (3,4), slope −3/4', color: 'red' }]}
/>

Differentiate both sides:

$$
2x + 2y\,\frac{dy}{dx} = 0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{dy}{dx} = -\frac{x}{y}
$$

At point $(3,4)$: slope $= -\tfrac{3}{4}$.

> **Quant Finance:** Extracting implied volatility from option prices; yield curve bootstrapping.

### 3.6 Derivatives of Logarithmic Functions

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{d}{dx}[\ln(x)] &= \frac{1}{x} \quad &(x > 0) \\
\frac{d}{dx}[\ln|x|] &= \frac{1}{x} \quad &(x \neq 0) \\
\frac{d}{dx}[\log_b(x)] &= \frac{1}{x \cdot \ln(b)} &
\end{aligned}
$$

**Visual:** $\frac{d}{dx}[\ln(x)] = \frac{1}{x}$

<HandPlot
  title="ln(x) and its derivative 1/x"
  xLabel="x" yLabel="y"
  domain={[0.05, 6]}
  range={[-2, 4]}
  curves={[
    { f: (x) => Math.log(x), color: 'ink', label: 'ln(x) — crosses at (1,0)' },

  ]}
  marks={[{ x: 1, label: 'x=1' }]}
/>

**Logarithmic Differentiation** — take ln of both sides first:
$$
\begin{aligned}
y &= x^x \\
\ln(y) &= x \cdot \ln(x) \\
\frac{y'}{y} &= \ln(x) + 1 \\
y' &= x^x(\ln(x) + 1)
\end{aligned}
$$

- $C(x)$ = total cost to produce $x$ units

$$
\frac{dy}{dt} = ky \quad \implies \quad y(t) = y_0 \cdot e^{kt}
$$

> This ODE says: "rate of change is proportional to current value" — bacteria, compound interest, radioactive decay, etc.

### 3.10 Linearization and Differentials

**Linear approximation (tangent line approximation):**

$$
L(x) = f(a) + f'(a)(x - a)
$$

<HandPlot
  title="Linearization: L(x) tangent to f(x) at x = a"
  xLabel="x" yLabel="y"
  domain={[0, 4]}
  range={[0, 8]}
  curves={[
    { f: (x) => x*x*0.5, color: 'ink', label: 'curve f(x)' },
    { f: (x) => 2*(x - 2) + 2, color: 'blue', dash: true, label: 'linear approx L(x)' },
  ]}
  points={[{ x: 2, y: 2, label: '(a, f(a))', color: 'red' }]}
  marks={[{ x: 2, label: 'a' }]}
/>

$L(x) \approx f(x)$ near $x = a$.

**Differential:**
$$
dy = f'(x) dx
$$

> When $x$ changes by $dx$, $y$ changes by approximately $dy$.

### 3.11 Hyperbolic Functions

$$
\sinh(x) = \frac{e^{x} - e^{-x}}{2}, \qquad
\cosh(x) = \frac{e^{x} + e^{-x}}{2}, \qquad
\tanh(x) = \frac{\sinh(x)}{\cosh(x)}
$$
