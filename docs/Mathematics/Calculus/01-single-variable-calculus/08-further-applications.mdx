### 8.1 Arc Length

Length of the curve $y=f(x)$ from $a$ to $b$:

$$
L = \int_a^b \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2}\, dx
$$

**Derivation.** Each tiny piece of arc has length

$$
ds = \sqrt{dx^2 + dy^2} = \sqrt{1 + \left(\tfrac{dy}{dx}\right)^2}\, dx,
\qquad
L = \int ds
$$

(the sum of all tiny arc elements $ds$ along the curve).

### 8.2 Surface Area of Revolution

$$
S = 2\pi \int_a^b f(x) \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2} \, dx
$$

**Each ring has:**
- $\text{radius} = f(x)$
- $\text{slant width} = \sqrt{1+[f'(x)]^2} \, dx$
- $\text{circumference} = 2\pi \cdot f(x)$

### 8.5 Probability

A **probability density function** $f(x)$ satisfies:

1. $f(x) \ge 0$ for all $x$
2. $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x)\, dx = 1$ (total probability $= 1$)
3. $\displaystyle P(a \le X \le b) = \int_a^b f(x)\, dx$ (the area under the curve between $a$ and $b$)

$$
\mu = \int x\,f(x)\, dx
\qquad
\sigma^2 = \int (x-\mu)^2\,f(x)\, dx
$$

The **normal PDF** is the classic bell curve:

$$
f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}\, e^{-(x-\mu)^2 / 2\sigma^2}
$$
